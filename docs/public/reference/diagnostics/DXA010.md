# DXA010: Construction Authority Violation

**Status:** Shipped in `0.1.0-alpha`
**Default severity:** Warning
**Category:** `Domain.Architecture`
**Mechanism:** Analyzer-enforced

## Trigger

Flags direct construction or public factory use outside authorized substrate or configured construction boundaries.

## Remediation

Route creation through the configured construction boundary.

## Limits

Reflection, serialization, and indirect construction are outside complete coverage. Standard compiler suppression mechanisms are technically available; consuming repositories decide whether policy permits them.

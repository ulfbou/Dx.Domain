# DXA011: Public Factory Exposure

**Status:** Shipped in `0.1.0-alpha`
**Default severity:** Warning
**Category:** `Domain.Architecture`
**Mechanism:** Analyzer-enforced

## Trigger

Flags public construction surfaces on classified domain types.

## Remediation

Make the surface non-public or expose creation through the approved boundary.

## Limits

Classification and indirection can limit detection. Standard compiler suppression mechanisms are technically available; consuming repositories decide whether policy permits them.
